How efficient sizing works

{{ product_name }} does not re-encode. The efficient size is what will be on disk because it downloads the smaller native rendition directly. Resolution and codec vary by video on purpose: efficient spends one playlist-wide budget, starts every video at the smallest legible rendition, and buys height upgrades where they cost the fewest bytes. Forcing one resolution would either waste the budget or throw away higher-resolution videos, and forcing one codec would require a re-encode. For a predictable size, the fixed caps (such as 720p max) take the best rendition at or below that height for every video. {% if job.in_library %}This playlist is already in your library; syncing adds new videos without touching existing media, and the refresh tick below also updates the details of the videos you already have.{% endif %}
